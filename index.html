Catering

Homemade, from farm to bowl

Every meal plan is cooked fresh for your pet. Here is what happens between a farmer's field and your pet's bowl.

Fresh from local farmers

We buy directly from farmers and fishermen around Jakarta: vegetables from Lembang, free-range chicken from Bogor, and the morning catch from Muara Angke. No middlemen and no long-haul frozen imports. Ingredients reach our kitchen within a day of harvest, and we pay our growers fairly for it.

Homemade with care, in our own kitchen

Every meal is cooked in small batches by our own cooks in our own kitchen. It isn't outsourced or made in a factory, and it contains no preservatives. We slow-cook and gently steam, the way you would at home, then portion each meal to the calories your nutritionist prescribed.

  1. Day 1 · 05:00

    Source

    We collect vegetables, meat and fish directly from partner farmers at dawn.

  2. Day 1 · 08:00

    Inspect

    Each delivery is checked, weighed and logged, then stored cold.

  3. Day 1 · 10:00

    Cook

    Our cooks prepare small batches from nutritionist-approved recipes.

  4. Day 1 · 14:00

    Portion

    Weighed to your pet's calorie target and labelled with their name.

  5. Day 1 · 16:00

    Seal & chill

    Vacuum-sealed and blast-chilled to 4 °C to lock in freshness.

  6. Day 2 · 07:00

    Deliver

    Packed in insulated boxes and at your door in your chosen morning window.

About us

Fauna, with a little aurora

Faunora started from a simple observation: most pet owners we spoke to were not sure their pet's food was right for them, and nobody was in a position to tell them.

So we kept it focused. First, a real consultation with a professional pet nutritionist. Second, food cooked in our own kitchen to match the plan, plus the treats, toys and clothes we would happily use on our own animals. We are not a marketplace. We do not resell a thousand brands. If it has the Faunora name on it, we made it, packed it, or picked it ourselves.

Our history

Founded in five minutes. Honestly.

Most founding stories involve a garage, a dream and a decade. Ours involves a deadline.

  1. 00:00

    A Venture Creation assignment notices us before we notice it. The deadline is uncomfortably close. Four students look at each other.

  2. 00:47

    Someone observes that pets eat food. This is accepted as a market insight.

  3. 02:10

    A name is chosen: Peybo. Nobody can explain it. Nobody tries.

  4. 03:30

    The feature list grows to include hotels, grooming, a marketplace and possibly the moon.

  5. 04:59

    Submitted. Exactly five minutes, start to finish. We would like to say it was strategy. It was the deadline.

  6. Later

    Our professor gently asked what we were actually good at. The answer was nutrition and food. We dropped the moon, kept the bowl, and renamed ourselves Faunora.

We still work fast. We just think first now.
